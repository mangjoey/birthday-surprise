Happy Birthday, Mommy!

Mommy, I wanted to make something different for you this year. I know I could have simply bought you another gift, but I wanted to make something myself — something that tells a little bit of our story.

Looking at these photos reminds me how much we've already been through together. From our first date, to our adventures, to getting married, and eventually becoming Mommy and Daddy to Jarren.

We've changed a lot since that first photo. Life became busier, we gained more responsibilities, and now most of our days aren't just about the two of us anymore. But through all of that, you're still the person I want beside me.

Thank you for being my wife, my partner, my Love, and my Asawa. Thank you for all the little things you do that I probably don't say thank you for enough.

And Mommy, thank you for being such an amazing mother to Jarren. Seeing the two of you together and seeing the way you love and care for him makes me appreciate you even more.

I know I'm not always the sweetest person and I don't always know how to express everything perfectly. Sometimes I may be serious, Minsan makulit ako, minsan annoying 😆 — but I hope you never doubt how important you are to me.

Because even the ordinary days are special when I get to come home to you and Jarren.

And if somehow I could go all the way back to the beginning, knowing everything that would happen — the good days, the difficult days, the adventures, the changes, and the life we have now...

I would still choose you, Love.

Again and again and again. ❤️

Happy Birthday, My Love. I love you, Mommy. ❤️
